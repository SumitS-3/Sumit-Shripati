  3. Verify the humidity sensor probe is clean and unobstructed.

6. Limitations and Uncertainties

  • Thresholds for spike and flatline detection are configurable estimates, not calibrated instrument specifications. Short flatlines (≤ 1 h) are flagged for awareness but may represent genuine stable atmospheric conditions.
  • Cross-station comparison is qualitative: stations differ in elevation and local microclimate, so agreement is expected but not guaranteed.
  • The maintenance log is from volunteer memory and may be incomplete or imprecise about exact times.
  • GPS position offsets are within GPS accuracy for most stations and do not indicate significant location errors.
  • Rain-gauge blockage periods (e.g. S04 before 2025-04-02) cannot be distinguished from genuine dry periods without cross-station rainfall comparison.
